                                     ABOUT JIPMER


 Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research (JIPMER),
   Puducherry, under the Government of India since the year 1956, is one of the leading
   Medical Institutions of India. Spread over a sprawling 195-acre campus in an urban locale
   of Puducherry (formerly Pondicherry), JIPMER is 170 km by road from Chennai.
 JIPMER was declared as an “Institution of National Importance” by an Act of Parliament,
   JIPMER, Puducherry, Act, 2008. A copy of the Act was Gazette notified on 14-7-2008 to
   enforce this Act. Prior to this, the Institute was functioning under the administrative control
   of the Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, New
   Delhi.
 The Institution is now empowered to award Medical Degrees under the clauses 23 & 24 of
   the said Act. Such Degrees shall be deemed to be included in the schedules to the respective
   Acts governing the Medical Council of India/National Medical Commission, Indian Nursing
   Council, and Dental Council of India, entitling the holders to the same privileges as those
   attached to the equivalent awards from the recognized Universities of India.
 JIPMER imparts Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG) and Super Specialty Medical
   Training through a large hospital complex (JIPMER Hospital) and a Nursing College. Some
   of the courses offered are MBBS, BSc, MSc, MD, MS, DM, MCh, PDCC, and PDF Courses.
   Full-time Ph.D. Programs are available in several disciplines.

                              SPONSORED CATEGORY

        Candidates applying for admission as a sponsored candidate should fulfill the
following conditions duly certified by his/her sponsoring authority employer for admission
to the course in the discipline. The candidate is required to submit the scanned copy of
sponsorship certificate (Annexure-1).
        In case of inability to upload the sponsorship certificate at the time of submission of
online application, applicants are permitted to upload scanned copy of sponsorship certificate
(.jpg /pdf) using his/her user id and password on or before 18.05.2025 (Monday) 05:00 PM
in thedatabase. Non-receipt of scanned copy in the database of such candidate will lead to
automatic rejection of his/her application and hall ticket will not be generated/issued. The
sponsored certificate should contain the following declaration by the sponsoring authority.
       That the candidate concerned is a permanent or regular employee of the deputing /
        sponsoring authority, and should have been working for at least last three years (on
        or before 30th June 2026).
     and that the candidate, after training at JIPMER, Puducherry, will be suitably
        employed by the deputing/sponsoring authority to work for at least five years in the
        specialty in which training is received by the candidate at JIPMER.
     and that no financial implications, in the form of emoluments/stipend etc. will
        devolve upon JIPMER, Puducherry during the entire period of his/her course and
        such payment shall be the responsibility of the sponsoring authority.
Sponsorship of candidates holding tenure appointments (like house job, Junior or Senior
Residency, adhoc or contract or honorary appointment against a leave vacancy) shall NOT be
accepted. Sponsorship of any candidate by private hospitals, Institutes or nursing homes are
not accepted. The sponsoring Institute should not nominate more than one candidate for each
of the specialties specified in the distribution of seats in the discipline. (Page No: 6 & 7)
Sponsorship / Deputation of candidates will be accepted only from the following:
   Central or State Government Departments/Institutions
   Autonomous Bodies of the Central or State Government
   Public sector colleges affiliated to universities and recognized by the MCI. In case of
    candidate deputed/ sponsored by the Medical College affiliated to Universities and
    recognized by National Medical Commission, deputation/sponsorship certificate signed
    by ONLY the Principal of Medical College concerned shall be accepted.

SELECTION:
       Sponsored/Deputed candidates are also required to appear and qualify in the common
entrance examination. If selected for admission to any course of the JIPMER, candidates are
required to make their own arrangement for stay during the period of their course.
       The sponsored candidates selected for admission will be granted only 1-month
time to produce Sponsorship/Relieving/Study leave/NOC certificates from the date of
documents verification. No further extension will be allowed under any circumstances.

                              PAYMENT FAILURE CASES

When a payment fails while paying the application fees, there could be two possibilities.
1. The amount is not debited –
In this case, ‘Payment failure’ message will be displayed on the screen and an SMS will
normally be received by the candidate from his/her bank stating that the payment could not
be processed.
2. The amount is debited –
In this case, an email may be sent to superspecialtyjipmer@gmail.com to know the status
of that payment.
Possible Reason(s) for Payment Failure:
Poor internet connection, Authorization failure, Authentication failure – Wrong details
entered, Delayed notification, Payment gateway failure, etc.
When a transaction fails on the receiver's side, but the payment gets debited from the user's
account, the amount gets automatically refunded by the bank within 10-15 working days. Most
banks do not notify users in case of refunds, so the candidates are advised to check with your
bank statement to ensure that the refund has been credited.
In the meantime, the fees can be paid again.
If it takes a long period for refund, an email may be sent to superspecialtyjipmer@gmail.com
with the following details:
1. Application sequence No/User ID:
2. Name of the Candidate              :
3. Transaction ID No                  :
The status of the refund will be intimated.
The refund will be made to the same account from which the payment was initiated by the
candidate.

METHOD OF RESOLVING TIES

If two or more candidates secure equal percentage in the entrance examination, their
    inter se merit shall be determined in the following order:

     1.   Negative Marks: - The candidate(s) who have fewer negative marks will be
          placed higher than the candidate(s) who have more negative marks.
     2.   Age: If the tie still exists, the candidate elder by age will be ranked higher.

RAGGING:

 Ragging is banned in this Institute. If a student is found to have indulged in ragging
  in the past, or if it is noticed later that he/she has indulged in ragging, then he/she
  may be expelled from the Institute.

SUPREME COURT RULES REGARDING ANTI RAGGING:
As per direction of the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India, the Government has banned ragging
completely in any form inside and outside the campus and all the JIPMER authorities are
determined not to allow any form of ragging. Whosoever directly or indirectly commits,
participates in, abets or instigates ragging within or outside JIPMER an FIR shall be lodged
against him/her and he/she will be suspended or rusticated from the institution and shall also
be liable to be fined which may extend to Rs.10,000/-. In case the applicant for admission is
found to have indulged in ragging in the past or if it is noticed later that he/she has indulged
in ragging, admission can be refused, or he/ she shall be expelled from the educational
institution. The punishment may also include suspension from attending the classes,
withholding/withdrawing stipend of PDF & PDC Courses/ scholarship and other financial
benefits or withholding the result.

 LEGAL JURISIDICTION: If any person(s) or invigilator(s) engaged in the conduct of
JIPMER Entrance Examination is found acting in a manner that would result in the leakage
of the question paper(s) or attempt to use or help in the use of unfair means in this
examination, he/she shall be liable for prosecution under the Indian Penal Code. The disputes,
if any, about admission process after the Entrance Examination, etc. will be subjectto the legal
