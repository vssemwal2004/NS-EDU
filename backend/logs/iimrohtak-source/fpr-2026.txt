Foreword                                                                                           3




The story of IIM Rohtak is one of continuous growth, resilience, and excellence. Here, success
is not just measured by placements but by the value we create for both our students and
the industry. The Final Placement Report for the PGP 2024-26 cohort stands as a testament
to this philosophy, marking yet another year of outstanding achievements. This year’s
placement season has not only reinforced our legacy of 100% placements but has also set
new benchmarks in opportunities, industry collaborations, and global recognition.

These accomplishments are a reflection of the unwavering trust of our valued recruiters,
who continue to recognise and reward the exceptional talent nurtured within our campus.
We extend our deepest gratitude to our recruiting partners, both long-standing and new, for
their continued confidence in our talent pool. The growing number of marquee recruiters
and pre-placement offers is a testament to the industry’s confidence in IIM Rohtak’s ability
to shape professionals equipped for real-world challenges.

At IIM Rohtak, placements are more than just an annual milestone; they embody the
collective spirit of an institution that nurtures talent, fosters ambition, and develops future
leaders. The unwavering commitment of our faculty, alumni, and placement team has been
instrumental in strengthening our mission to shape professionals who not only thrive in the
corporate world but also drive meaningful impact.

This report encapsulates more than just statistics, it tells the stories of aspirations,
determination, and success. As we continue to push boundaries and redefine excellence, we
invite you to witness the remarkable journeys that define the future of IIM Rohtak.




                                                                       Final Placement Report   2024-26
Director’s Message                                                                                 4




                       Prof. Dheeraj Sharma
                       Director


                       I am pleased to announce the successful completion of the Final
                       Placement process for the MBA Batch of 2024–26 at the Indian
                       Institute of Management Rohtak. I have been informed that all
                       eligible students have been successfully placed, resulting in 100%
placements, reaffirming the strong confidence that recruiters place in the capabilities,
values, and industry readiness of our graduates. The continued support of our valued
recruiting partners, coupled with the addition of several new organizations, resulted in a
vibrant and competitive placement process. Students secured opportunities across a
broad spectrum of roles in consulting, finance, marketing, operations, analytics, human
resources, product management, and general management.
Over the years, IIM Rohtak has fostered an ecosystem that promotes academic excellence,
professional competence, and leadership development. Through industry interactions, live
projects, management conclaves, industrial visits, workshops, and certification
programmes, students gain practical exposure to contemporary business practices and
emerging industry trends. Supported by accomplished faculty, a rigorous curriculum, and
industry-oriented pedagogy, they develop the managerial skills and insights needed to
excel in today’s dynamic business environment.
This year, the Institute further expanded its corporate outreach through associations with
several new recruiters, including BlackRock, Haleon, JSW Energy, Kotak Securities, Morgan
Stanley, Suzlon, and many other reputed organizations. Prominent recruiters participating
in the placement process included Aditya Birla Capital, Amazon, Amul, Barclays, AB InBev,
Capgemini, Cognizant, Ernst & Young, HSBC, ICICI Bank, IDBI Bank, Indus Valley Partners,
Kirloskar Oil Engines, SBI Capital Markets, NatWest, and Wells Fargo, among others.
The placement outcomes achieved by the MBA Batch of 2024–26 reflect the dedication
and perseverance of the students. The highest domestic compensation offered during the
placement season stood at ₹37.10 LPA, while the average compensation reached
₹20.03LPA, registering a growth of ~7% over the previous year’s average package. The
median compensation was recorded at ₹18.00 LPA. Over 40 Pre-Placement Offers were
extended to students, reflecting their strong internship performance and employers’
confidence in their potential. These achievements reflect the collective efforts of students,
faculty, alumni, recruiters, and staff. I extend my sincere appreciation to our recruiting
partners for their continued support and trust, which inspires us to further strengthen
management education and talent development at IIM Rohtak. I also acknowledge the
dedicated efforts of the Placement Committee in ensuring the successful conduct of the
placement process. As our graduating students begin their professional journeys, I am
confident they will uphold the Institute’s values and make meaningful contributions to
their organizations and society. IIM Rohtak remains committed to nurturing responsible,
innovative, and globally competent leaders.
I congratulate the MBA Batch of 2024–26 on this remarkable achievement and extend my
best wishes for their future success.

Message from Chairperson                                                                         5




                      Dr. Rubina Chakma
                      Placement Chair

                       With immense pleasure, I am happy to inform you that the final
                       placement for the academic year 2025-26 has been concluded
                       with the exceptional enthusiasm and proactivity of all the
                       involved stakeholders. The active participation of 210+ recruiters
                       has reinforced IIM Rohtak’s growing credibility as a reliable,
trustworthy and responsible management institution. The highest CTC of this year
reached INR 37.10 LPA, while the average and median CTCs stood at INR 20.03 LPA and
INR 18 LPA, respectively, reflecting a steady year-on-year average CTC increase of 6.9%.
With a remarkable PPOs (40+) secured, our students have demonstrated a high level of
industry readiness, reflecting the continued ability of the institute to deliver enduring
credibility and value to its legacy recruiters. It is also a moment of immense pride that
the placement team has strengthened its outreach, asevidenced by the active
engagement of 130+ new recruiters in the final placement processes. I want to express
my deep gratitude to all the recruiters for their continued trust and confidence, and the
institute looks forward to nurturing and deepening its relationships with both the
legacy and new recruiters. The remarkable placement outcome demonstrates the
commitment, consistent efforts, and dedication of the Placement Committee, Industry
Relations and Interaction Cell (IRIC), and Placement Preparation Committee. The
exemplary leadership, support, and guidance of the competent authority,
complemented by the insights of the Deans and faculty members, have been
instrumental in shaping the overall placement outcome. Lastly, I convey my best
wishes to our 15th PGP batch for their professional journeys and career growth.
