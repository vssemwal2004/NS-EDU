IIT Madras (IITM) Overview
About IIT Madras (IITM)

Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras or IITM) has been given the title of "Institute of Eminence". IIT Madras is a prestigious institution of national importance and a public technical university established in 1959. IIT Madras is a residential institute with around 550 faculty, 8000 students and 1250 administrative & supporting staff.  

IIT Madras Ranking 2027: #170 as per QS World Ranking 2027 with a score of 59.3. 

IIT Madras QS Subject Ranking 2026: #62 in Engineering & Technology; #156 in Natural Sciences and #79 in Computer Science. 
IITM Recognitions & Accreditations: Recognised as an Institute of National Importance. IITM Department of Mathematics has been awarded Silver Level recognition under the CAS University Recognition Programme by the Casualty Actuarial Society (CAS). 
Some of the Indian Institute of Technology Madras courses are BTech, BS, BSc, MTech, MBA, MA, MS, PhD and many more.
JEE Advanced, GATE, JAM and CAT scores are accepted for IITM admissions.
890 UG and 943 PG students got placed in IIT Madras 2024-25 placements as per the NIRF 2026 overall report.

IIT Madras has 16 departments, a few advanced research centres and around 100 laboratories organised in a unique pattern. IITM courses are offered in full-time, online, and part-time modes. IIT Madras courses are offered at UG, PG and doctoral levels. For B.Tech admissions at IIT Madras, candidates need a valid rank in the JEE Advanced exam. M.Tech admissions in IIT Madras are based on GATE scores. 

IIT Madras admissions for M.Sc courses are based on JAM examination scores. CAT/ GMAT scores are accepted for MBA admissions. IIT Madras fees for MSc is Rs 1.52 lakhs. Candidates must meet the IIT Madras cutoff 2025 to secure admission.

Why is IIT Madras So Famous?

Indian Institute of Technology Madras is nationally and internationally known for excellence in technical education, basic and applied research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and industrial consultancy. IIT Madras holds the distinction of being India's No.1 Engineering University by NIRF. IIT Madras was awarded the title of Institute of Eminence. Check below for IIT Madras ranking, cutoff and placement highlights. 

IIT Madras Research Park (IITMRP), India's first university-based research park, is spread across 11.42 acres and has over 1.2 million square feet of workspace with several formal, informal, and theme-based meeting and networking lounges to promote and sustain innovation through Industry-Academia collaboration. IIT Madras (IITM) Scholarship

IIT Madras offers various scholarships that include Merit-cum Means Scholarship, Institute National Prize, Indian Women’s Association at Bonn Scholarship, Aditya Birla Scholarship, ST Engineering Scholarship and many other scholarships are offered by the institute. 

These scholarships and fellowships are provided either for students with exemplary academic record as a means to appreciate their hard work or to students who belong to the economically weaker sections of the society so as to motivate them for higher education. Some of the IIT Madras scholarships are Institute Notional Prize, ST Engineering Scholarship, Indian Women's Association at Bonn Scholarship, among a few others. IIT Madras also helps students requiring financial help with selective scholarship amounts and fee waiver.

IIT Madras (IITM) Admission

Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IITM) offers admissions to BTech, BS, MSc, MA, MTech, MSc, MS, MBA and many other programmes. Students have to fulfil the basic eligibility criteria to apply for IIT Madras admission.


Admissions at IIT Madras are offered based on the performance of students in entrance exams.
Entrance Exams accepted: JEE Advanced for BTech admissions, GATE for MTech admissions, JAM for MSc admissions and CAT scores are accepted for the IIT Madras MBA admission process. 
JoSAA counselling is conducted for BTech admissions and COAP counselling for MTech admissions. 
GATE 2027 application process will start on August 14, 2026.

JEE Advanced scores are considered for IIT Madras admission to the B.Tech course. For the M.Tech course, students have to appear and qualify for the GATE examination. CAT scores are considered for MBA admissions. GMAT scores are considered for foreign students. Students willing to apply for admission to the Indian Institute of Technology Madras online degree programme can visit the official website and fill out the application form. To complete the IIT Madras admission process, the candidates should submit the required documents and pay the admission fee. Students can pursue both offline and online IIT Madras courses in several disciplines.

IIT Madras B.Tech Admissions 2026

Indian Institute of Technology Madras offers B.Tech courses in different specialisations, including B.Tech Mechanical Engineering, B.Tech Electrical Engineering, B.Tech Civil Engineering, B.Tech Chemical Engineering, B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering and others. JEE Advanced scores are considered for IIT Madras B.Tech selection process. Qualified students must then attend JoSAA counselling as per IIT Madras admission process for B.Tech courses. The details of IIT Madras B.Tech admission criteria are given below. 

IIT Madras BTech Admission Process

Students must meet IIT Madras BTech eligibility criteria

Aspiring students need to enrol and appear for both JEE Main.

JEE Main qualified students must then appear and qualify for JEE Advanced.

Students must acquire an All India Rank (AIR) for counselling invitations.

Students must register for the Joint Seat Allocation counselling process (JoSAA).

During counselling, students must select IIT Madras as the institution of choice. 

IIT Madras admissions to UG courses are based on performance in JEE Advanced. 

After selection, students must visit the institute to collect the provisional letter of admission.

Final IIT Madras admission will be offered after document verification and IIT Madras admission fees payment.

IIT Madras BS Degree Admissions 2026

IIT Madras offers the IIT Madras BS Degree with flexibility to exit at any level. Based on the courses completed and credits earned, the learner can receive a Foundation Certificate from IITM CODE (Centre for Outreach and Digital Education) or Diploma(s) from IIT Madras or BSc Degree in Programming and Data Science from IIT Madras or BS Degree in Data Science and Applications from IIT Madras.

IIT Madras BS Data Science and Applications Selection Process 

All regular entry applicants must go through the Qualifier Process for admission to the Foundation Level for the IIT Madras BS programme. 

The qualifier process mainly includes 4 weeks of coursework based on lecture videos, assignments, and live sessions of the four foundational level courses - English I, Mathematics for Data Science I, Statistics for Data Science I, and Computational Thinking - that will be provided through our online portal. Every week, an assignment must be submitted for grading in each course.

Based on the content covered in the 4 weeks of study, at the end of the 4 weeks, a qualifier exam will be conducted for eligible candidates.

Students eligible to appear for the most recent JEE Advanced are directly admitted to the Foundation Level.

IIT Madras Admissions 2026 for Dual Degree Courses

Bachelor of Technology and Master of Technology in Engineering Design (B.Tech+M.Tech) is a full-time Dual Degree engineering programme. BS+MS is also a dual degree course offered by IIT Madras in 2 specialisations, including BS and MS Biological Sciences Dual Degree and BS and MS Physics Dual Degree. JEE Advanced scores are considered for IIT Madras admissions to B.Tech+M.Tech and BS+MS. Given below are details of IIT Madras selection process for dual degree courses. 

IIT Madras Admission Process for B.Tech+M.Tech/BS+MS

Satisfy IIT Madras eligibility criteria for dual degree programmes. 

Students have to appear and qualify for the JEE Main and JEE Advanced.  

After the declaration of results, register for the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA). 

Choose IIT Madras as the first choice.  

Shortlisting of candidates for IITM admissions is done based on choice filled, JEE Advanced score and category.

Once selected for IIT Madras admission, visit the campus.

Obtain a provisional letter of admission from IIT Madras with admission details and tuition fees.

Submit the required documents and pay the IIT Madras tuition fees to confirm admission.

IIT Madras MSc Admissions 2026

Indian Institute of Technology Madras admissions to M.Sc courses are offered in 3 specialisations:M.Sc Chemistry, M.Sc Physics and M.Sc Mathematics. According to the IIT Madras M.Sc selection process, students must have valid JAM scores. The details of IIT Madras M.Sc selection criteria are given below.

Eligible candidates should enrol and appear for the JAM test.

Secure the desired JAM score in the All India Rank List according to the cut-off.

The candidate should attend JAM counselling.

Candidates will be shortlisted for IIT Madras admissions based on the scores obtained in the entrance examination.

The selected candidates will be notified.

To complete the IIT Madras admission process, the candidates should pay the IIT Madras admission fees.

IIT Madras M.Tech Admissions 2026

GATE scores are considered for IIT Madras M.Tech selection process. Candidates graduating/ graduated from IITs with B.Tech. Degree and having CGPA of 8.0 (on a scale of 10) and above can apply without a GATE Score as per the admission process of IIT Madras. The details of IIT Madras M.Tech selection criteria are given below. 

IIT Madras M.Tech Admission Process 

Candidates seeking admission will have to apply through the official website of the university. 

While filling out the application, candidates must attach all the necessary documents and pay the IIT Madras application fee. 

Students must have secured valid marks in GATE.

The cutoff and date of the interview will be released by the institute itself. 

All M.Tech. Admission offers (through GATE) will be displayed on the Common Offer Acceptance Portal (COAP). Candidates must register at the COAP portal to view and accept their offers. 

After the interviews and after the students have made their final choice, admission letters will be handed out to the final selected candidates.

IIT Madras Direct Admission Process for M.Tech Courses

IIT Graduates with a CGPA of 8.0 or above are exempted from having a GATE score. 

Eligible students must fill out the IIT Madras application form from the official website.

Students will be selected for direct M.Tech admissions based on an interview. 

Finally, get documents verified and pay IIT Madras fees.

IIT Madras MBA Admissions 2026

Students will be shortlisted for IIT Madras MBA admissions based on CAT scores. Candidates can check their IIT Madras shortlist status 2025 by entering their registered email ID and password for MBA admission. The IIT Madras PI shortlist is prepared based on CAT score 2024. The DOMS IIT Madras shortlisted candidates can attend the writing and interview to qualify for the final round of the admission process. After the interview, the institute will publish the final IIT Madras MBA shortlist. Check out the details on IIT Madras MBA selection process.

IIT Madras MBA Admission Process

Candidates willing to take the IIT Madras MBA course must appear for the CAT/GMAT. 

After securing a valid CAT score, students need to appear for CAP and the counselling process.

Shortlisting of candidates is done for IIT Madras admissions based on the students taking the CAT scores and the counselling process into consideration. 

Selected candidates will be notified through the registered mobile ID/phone number.

Final selected candidates must submit the required documents along with the IIT Madras admission fees. 

IIT Madras MBA Shortlist Criteria 

Students are shortlisted for IIT Madras MBA admission based on their academic performance, work experience, score of CAT 2024 score, and PI score. Shortlisted candidates can attend the interview round at the allotted interview centres.

How Do I Check the IIT Madras Shortlist Status?

Candidates who qualified for the CAT exam can check the IIT Madras MBA PI status as per the steps mentioned below. Shortlisted candidates must attend the reflective writing and interview rounds. 

Visit the DOMS IIT Madras MBA official website

Click on the IIT Madras MBA shortlist.

Enter the registered email ID and password.

Students can view the IIT Madras MBA shortlist status.

DOMS IIT Madras shortlisted candidates can download the IIT Madras PI shortlist 2025 pdf for future reference.

IIT Madras MS Admissions 2026

The institute offers MS in specialisations including MS by Research Entrepreneurship, MS Metallurgical and Material Engineering, MS Mechanical Engineering, MS Computer Science and Engineering, MS Management, MS Electrical Engineering and many more. Students can check below details of the IIT Madras MS selection process below.

IIT Madras MS Admission Process 

Candidates seeking admission must apply by filling out the IIT Madras online application form available on the official website of the institute. 

While filling out the application, applicants must upload all the required documents and pay the IIT Madras fee.

Eligible candidates will be called for a written test followed by a round of interviews.

Selected students must pay the IIT Madras admission fees for the MS programme.
