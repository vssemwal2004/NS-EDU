Top B.Tech Colleges in India 2025 - Ranking, Fees, Admission, Placements 2025
Written By Amit Singh - Content Writer
Updated on - Oct 08, 2026 05:54 AM IST
Top B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) colleges in India are IIT Madras, IIT Delhi, IIT Bombay, IIT Kanpur, IIT Roorkee, IIT Kharagpur, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, NIT Trichy, Jadavpur University. About 4405 colleges in India offer B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) in multiple specialisations. Out of these, 225 are run by the government and 2892 are privately owned. Admission to top B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) colleges in India is conducted through various entrance exams like SAEEE, JAC Chandigarh, MPBSE 10th, VTUEEE, MPBSE 12th, TBSE 12th, Karnataka 2nd PUC, SNUET, JEE Main, NTA. The fees for top B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) colleges in India range from INR 1,000 - INR 63,75,450.
