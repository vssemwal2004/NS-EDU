FAQs 

Q. Which is the No. 1 BTech College in India?

A. IIT Madras is ranked 1st on the NIRF among engineering colleges across India. 

Q. Which institute is best for BTech?

A. A popular choice among students is IIT Bombay ranking 3rd on the NIRF ranking.

Q. Which is better BTech or BE?

A. A BE degree deals with more theoretical knowledge whereas B.Tech courses focus on practical aspects of the subject prioritizing putting the theory into practice that being said students should decide for themselves what will suit them better in terms of what they want to do in the future with their education 

Q. Can we join B.Tech without JEE?

A. While most top colleges for B.E/B. Tech in India offers admission based on JEE exam Scores but some colleges accept various other state or institution-based entrance exam 

Q. Who is eligible for BTech after 12th?

A. Candidates must have passed their 10+2 or equivalent exam with a minimum of 50 percent marks from an accredited board and have studied Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as core subjects.

Commonly Asked Questions
Top B.E /B.Tech Colleges in India
Q:
What are the eligibility criteria for B.E/B.Tech admission in India?
A:
To be eligible for B.E/B.Tech admission in India, candidates must have: - Passed 10+2 or equivalent exam with a minimum of 50% marks - Studied Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects - Valid score in entrance exams like JEE Main, JEE Advanced, BITSAT, VITEEE, SRMJEEE, COMEDK UGET, or state-level entrance tests
Q:
How do I apply for B.E/B.Tech programs in India?
A:
The application process for B.E/B.Tech programs in India typically involves: - Registering and appearing for the relevant entrance exam - Filling out the application form for the desired college/university - Submitting required documents like mark sheets, entrance exam score, etc. - Attending counseling rounds and seat allotment as per merit and preferences
Q:
What are the top B.E/B.Tech colleges in India?
A:
According to the NIRF 2023 rankings, the top 10 B.E/B.Tech colleges in India are: 1. IIT Madras 2. IIT Delhi 3. IIT Bombay 4. IIT Kanpur 5. IIT Roorkee 6. IIT Kharagpur 7. IIT Guwahati 8. IIT Hyderabad 9. NIT Trichy 10. Jadavpur University, Kolkata
Q:
What are the B.E/B.Tech programs offered at IIT Bombay?
A:
IIT Bombay offers the following B.E/B.Tech programs: - Aerospace Engineering - Biomedical Engineering - Chemical Engineering - Civil Engineering - Computer Science & Engineering - Electrical Engineering - Mechanical Engineering - Metallurgical Engineering - Mining Engineering - Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering
Q:
What is the B.E/B.Tech fee structure at IIT Bombay?
A:
The total fee for the 4-year B.E/B.Tech program at IIT Bombay is approximately Rs. 8,31,000, which includes: - Tuition fee: Rs. 2,00,000 - Hostel and Mess charges: Rs. 1,80,000 - Other fees: Rs. 4,51,000

What are the admission requirements for Chandigarh University's B.E/B.Tech programs?

Q:
What is the fee structure for B.E/B.Tech at Chandigarh University?

Q:
What kind of campus facilities are available at Chandigarh University?

Q:
What is the faculty profile at Chandigarh University?
