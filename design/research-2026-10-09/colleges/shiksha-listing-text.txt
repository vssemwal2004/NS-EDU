5016 Best BTech Colleges in India 2026
Shiksha lists  5,000+ engineering colleges in India, including government colleges like IITs, NITs and IIITs, and private ones like VIT, BITS Pilani, SRM and LPU. JEE Main is the main entrance exam, while other exams like BITSAT, COMEDK, WBJEE, MHT CET, KCET, VITEEE and SRMJEEE are also accepted by different colleges. IITs, NITs, IIITs and GFTIs need JEE scores, but many private universities give direct admission based on Class 12 marks. Before choosing an  engineering colleges in India, check the full four-year fee, real placement data, student reviews and AICTE approval.
