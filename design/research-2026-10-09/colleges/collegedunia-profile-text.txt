Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras) is an Institute of National Importance established in 1959 by the Government of India in collaboration with the former Federal Republic of Germany. It operates as an autonomous institute under the Ministry of Education, recognised by UGC, approved by AICTE for applicable programmes, and accredited with an ’A’ grade by NAAC. IIT Madras is not affiliated to any external university.

As per NIRF 2025, IIT Madras is ranked #1 in Engineering and #1 Overall in India. The institute has retained the top position in both categories for the tenth consecutive year, with a NIRF score of 88.72.

The 617-acre campus in Adyar, Chennai, sits within a notified forest reserve and is home to spotted deer, bonnet macaques, and over 250 bird species. It is one of the most ecologically distinctive urban campuses in India.

IIT Madras Admission Process
B.Tech and BS (via JEE Advanced)

Admission to B.Tech, BS, Dual Degree, and B.Tech + MBA programmes at IIT Madras is exclusively through JEE Advanced. Eligibility requirements are as follows.

Passed 10+2 or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects
Minimum 75% aggregate in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD candidates)
Must be among the top 2,50,000 JEE Main qualifiers to appear for JEE Advanced
Maximum two attempts at JEE Advanced

Selection stages are as follows: JEE Advanced qualifying rank, then JoSAA online registration and choice filling, mock allotment, seat allotment rounds (up to 5), document verification, and admission confirmation. For B.Tech CSE at IIT Madras, the JEE Advanced General Category closing rank has historically fallen in the range of AIR 50 to 100. Engineering Physics and Aerospace Engineering closing ranks typically fall in the AIR 200 to 600 range.

Online BSc (Data Science and Applications)

The online BSc programme operates on a separate admission track. Candidates with a Class 12 pass certificate (any stream) can apply through the IITM BS qualifier exam portal. There is no JEE Advanced requirement. The programme admits students at Qualifier, Foundation, Diploma, and Degree levels. Learners can exit at Diploma level or continue to full BS degree.

M.Tech and MS (Research)

M.Tech admission requires a valid GATE score in the relevant discipline. Candidates with high GATE scores are shortlisted for departmental interviews. MS (Research) candidates additionally appear for a Research Aptitude Test (RAT) conducted by IITM, followed by a technical interview. Sponsored candidates from industry may be admitted without GATE in some departments, subject to departmental rules.

MBA (DOMS)

The MBA programme at the Department of Management Studies (DOMS) admits candidates through valid CAT scores. Shortlisted candidates appear for a personal interview at IITM Chennai. The programme targets candidates with professional experience, with the typical batch profile including engineers and non-engineers from diverse backgrounds. The CAT cutoff for shortlisting is typically in the 97 to 99 percentile band for the General Category.

M.Sc and MA

M.Sc admissions are through IIT JAM scores in Physics, Chemistry, or Mathematics, followed by JoSAA-based counselling. MA admissions in Development Studies, Economics, English, and Public Policy are through JAM or a department-level aptitude test, depending on the discipline.

Ph.D.

Ph.D. candidates must hold a qualifying PG degree and a valid GATE, UGC NET, or CSIR NET score. IITM also conducts a Research Aptitude Test (RAT) for candidates without GATE or NET. Selected candidates go through a departmental interview before provisional admission. The institute offers a monthly fellowship to full-time Ph.D. scholars as per Ministry of Education norms.

IIT Madras Campus and Infrastructure

The IIT Madras campus spans 617 acres in Adyar, Chennai and is classified as an ecological hotspot. The campus is divided into three functional zones: Academic, Hostel, and Residential. It hosts 20 hostels, with 15 for male students and 5 for female students, all named after Indian rivers (Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Krishna, Alaknanda, among others).

Academic facilities include a Central Library with over 3.5 lakh volumes and access to international journals, a Computer Centre with 24x7 connectivity, and well-equipped research laboratories in every department. The Students’ Activities Centre (SAC) includes an Olympic-size swimming pool, a wood-floored indoor multipurpose stadium, a cricket ground, badminton courts, and a fully equipped gymnasium.

The campus hosts a dedicated Research Park (IITM Research Park), one of India’s largest technology parks associated with an academic institution, housing over 200 companies including multinational R&D centres. The Centre for Innovation (CFI) runs 20 plus student clubs in areas ranging from robotics and aerospace to electric vehicles and bioengineering, giving students access to fabrication labs and project funding year-round.

Can I get an IIT with 4000 JEE Advanced Rank?
Harish Sharma

4,000 JEE Advanced rank is genuinely a solid score — you're in the top ~22% of all qualified candidates and most IIT seats are within reach.

what you can realistically get (general category):

Good IITs in non-CSE branches: IIT Roorkee (Chemical, Civil, Mech, Electrical), IIT Guwahati (similar branches), IIT BHU/IIT Dhanbad (ECE, EE, Mech) are realistic
IIT Kharagpur, IIT Kanpur: some non-core branches might just open at 4000
IIT Delhi: very limited at 4000 general — some interdisciplinary programs (Maths & Computing, Engineering Physics) may open but CSE/ECE/EE won't
IIT Bombay, Madras: highly unlikely at 4000 general for any branch
Newer IITs (Bhilai, Goa, Dharwad): easy to get CSE/ECE here

one thing people miss — if you're female, the supernumerary seats at all IITs have more relaxed cutoffs than gender-neutral seats, so more doors open. similarly, if you're OBC/SC/ST check your category rank separately — 4000 AIR with a good category rank can get you significantly better options.

What is the HTRA & NHTRA scheme in IIT Madras?
Aditi Verma

so HTRA and NHTRA are two categories of admission for MS/PhD at IIT Madras — the main difference is whether you get a stipend or not.

HTRA (Half-Time Research Assistantship):

you get a monthly stipend: Rs.37,000/month for PhD (yrs 1-2), Rs.42,000 for yrs 3-5; Rs.12,400/month for MS
in exchange, you do 8 hours/week of teaching/research assistantship work for the department
requires valid GATE or UGC/CSIR NET score to be eligible
most competitive seats, selected via departmental interview/written test

NHTRA (No assistantship):

same program, same degree, same labs, same research supervisor
zero monthly stipend from the institute
candidates without GATE score or from sponsored/project categories usually fall here
can potentially convert to HTRA later if you meet eligibility criteria and get department recommendation

practical take — if you have GATE score, try hard for HTRA because Rs.37-42k/month essentially covers living expenses at IIT Madras. NHTRA is fine if you have external fellowship (JRF, INSPIRE etc.) or company sponsorship. same degree at the end of the day though.
