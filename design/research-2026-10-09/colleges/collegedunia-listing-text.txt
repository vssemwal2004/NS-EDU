BTech Colleges In India 2026: Fees, Admissions, Placements, Rankings, Cutoff

Shawon Roy Chowdhury

Content Strategist at Collegedunia | Updated on - Aug 27, 2026

IIT Delhi, IIT Bombay, IIT Madras, IIT Kanpur, and IIT Kharagpur are the top BTech colleges in India for 2026. IIT Delhi leads the Collegedunia BTech Rankings 2026 with a CD Score of 997 out of 1000, followed by IIT Bombay at 997 and IIT Madras at 992. Government BTech fees range from 8.35 Lakh at IIT BHU Varanasi to 25.41 Lakh at IIIT Hyderabad, while private fees range from 7.83 Lakh at VIT Vellore to 33.44 Lakh at IIIT Bangalore.

The average/median packages range from 14.01 LPA at NIT Trichy to 34.40 LPA at IIIT Hyderabad. IIT Kanpur offers the best ROI at 236%, with a median package of 21.70 LPA on fees of just 9.19 Lakh.

BTech Colleges in India: Admission 2026

Admission to the top BTech colleges in India is decided almost entirely by entrance rank. The IITs admit through JEE Advanced, the NITs and IIITs through JEE Main, and BITS through BITSAT, while a common Class 12 threshold of 75% (65% for SC/ST) applies across the IIT system.
