About IIT Madras

Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IITM) was established in 1959. As per the QS World University Ranking, IIT Madras was ranked 180 with a score of 58.4. IIT Madras campus is spread across 630 acres of land and has over 550 faculty, 9700 students and 1250 administrative & supporting staff. IITM courses are spread across 16 academic departments. It offers multiple courses at the undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral levels. Apart from the regular courses, it also offers an online B.Sc programme. IIT Chennai admissions in all these programmes are done based on entrance exams. UG admissions are done through JEE Advanced, and admission to the MA and M.Tech programmes are done through GATE. Candidates willing to take admission in the MBA and M.Sc programme need to appear for CAT and JAM respectively.

The placement at IITM is quite impressive. It has the best placement records for various courses in the country. The highest package at IITM for the current year was recorded more than 4 Crores per annum with an average of 20 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visited IIT Madras for placements included Apple, Google, Texas Instruments, Qualcomm, Bajaj Auto Ltd., Microsoft, and JP Morgan Chase & Co. The IIT Chennai infrastructure facilities are as per the industry standards and all the necessary steps are taken to provide the students with the perfect learning environment. Banks, hospitals, shopping complex, bus transport, canteen, hostels, sports complexes, etc are some of the top IITM facilities.

IIT Madras has consistently ranked as the top institution in India according to the NIRF rankings. It retained its No.1 ranking in the Overall category for the sixth consecutive year and in the Engineering category for the ninth consecutive year. This performance underscores its strong academic programs and research capabilities. Admissions to IIT Madras are based on national level entrance exams. BTech and MTech admissions for this year have already concluded, GATE and CAT examination registration is all set to begin in August 2025, CAT is scheduled on November 30, 2025.

IIT Madras Courses and Admissions 2026

IIT Madras has 16 academic departments and a few advanced research centres in various disciplines of pure sciences and engineering. The curriculum of IIT Madras is designed in a way to encourage students to balance both academics and research. Several undergraduate, postgraduate, integrated, and doctoral-level courses are available at IIT Madras. It provides B.Tech and dual degree programmes in twelve different disciplines. The dual degree (BS & MS) in biological sciences and physics is available. MA, M.Sc, M.Tech, and MBA programmes are available at IIT Madras at the postgraduate level. Other IIT Madras courses available at the postgraduate level include the AICTE-sponsored QIP, the EMBA programme, and the web-enabled M.Tech programme. There are also numerous research programmes available, such as the Joint PhD and AICTE QIP. For all the courses, the IITM fees is different. IIT Madras Application Process 2025
First and foremost, candidates must meet the eligibility criteria set for the courses.
Candidates then need to apply, appear and qualify the respective entrance exams. For example, for admission into M.Tech and MA programme, candidates need to clear the GATE, and register in the COAP portal as the COAP registration number and scores is necessary to fill up the IITM application form
Once the above two points are cleared, candidates need to visit the IIT Madras official website.
Candidates then need to fill up the application form and attach the necessary documents. Candidates then need to pay the IITM application fee. Candidates must note that the application fee will vary category wise.
After the application is submitted and the fee is paid, candidates must keep a copy of the filled application form a well as the payment slip for future reference. This will mark the end of the IITM application process.

IIT Madras Placement

Indian Institute of Technology Madras placement report 2024-25 is out. As per the IIT Madras placement report 2024, a total of 256 companies visited the campus for placements, and a total of 1,612 offers were made. 1,091 students were placed with an average package of INR 22 LPA (B.Tech). IIT Chennai placement cell looks after the overall functioning from inviting recruiters, conducting pre-placement talks to organising the placement drive. The placement cell at Indian Institute of Technology Madras also conducts necessary training and workshops to make the students interview ready. The placement statistics for IIT Madras in 2024 indicate that approximately 80% of BTech and dual degree students and 75% of MTech students have been successfully placed. Notable recruiters include Apple, Google, Texas Instruments, and Qualcomm.
