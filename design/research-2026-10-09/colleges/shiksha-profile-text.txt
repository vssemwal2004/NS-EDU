IIT Madras Highlights 2026

IIT Madras is known as an ‘Institute of Eminence'. The Indian Institute of Technology Madras aka IITM was established as a public technical & research university. It is the third IIT to be set up by the Government of India & is autonomous in nature. IIT Madras is located on Sardar Patel Road, Adyar, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. A few more interesting highlights regarding IIT Madras Chennai are listed below:

IIT Madras Ranking: NIRF ranking 2026 is not out yet. However, students can refer previous year's data. IIT Madras is #1 Engineering institute based on NIRF 2025 ranking. The institute also topped the Overall category of NIRF 2025. QS World Rankings 2027 placed IITM at #170 globally. The institute climbed up 10 places compared to 2026 rankings under the same category. IITM has been ranked 1 under the Engineering category by NIRF 2025.
Popular Programmes: IIT Madras is a residential institute, which offers BTech, MTech, MBA, MSc, BS, Dual Degrees (BTech + MTech/ BS + MS), and more. BTech and MTech are the most popular programme of IIT Madras.
Fees: IIT Madras Fees for both IIT Madras BTech & BSc courses are INR 8 lakhs (tuition fees only). For MTech, the tuition fee is INR 1 lakh.
IIT Madras Placements: For BTech departments, such as EE, CS and CH, the average package recorded was INR 28.8 LPA, INR 53.2 LPA, INR 18.8 LPA, respectively in 2025 placements. MBA and MBA Tech recorded an average package of INR 17.9 LPA & INR 20 LPA, respectively.
IIT Madras admission criteria is entirely entrance-based. For its BTech & programme, JEE Main + JEE Advanced + JoSAA counselling is required. While MTech admissions are through GATE + COAP. IIT JAM is mandatory for MSc admission. The application and admission mode is online.
JEE Advanced Cutoff 2026: Under the flagship BTech course, CSE turned out to be the most competitive specialisation as per IIT Madras cutoff JEE Adavanced 2026. The Round 4 closing cutoff for the same in 2026 was 150 for the General AI category. BTech in Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering is the easiest BTech branch to get into, with a closing Gen AI rank of 7837.
IIT Madras Scholarship: The institute offers merit-based and need-based scholarships , where students can up to a 67% tuition fee waiver.
Campus: The Indian Institute of Technology Madras campus is home to India’s first university-based research park- IITMR. IIT Madras Research Park (IITMRP) aims to build a knowledge & innovation ecosystem.
Recent Collaboration: OpenAI has recently announced its first Learning Accelerator in India in collaboration with IIT Madras, Chennai. The college has also collaborated with the LSU Health New Orleans.

IIT Madras Cutoff 2026

IIT Madras cutoff 2026 is out for JEE Advanced Round 5. For Round 5, the opening and closing ranks ranged from 151 to 6440.

For Round 4, the IIT Madras cutoff trends reflected a high competition, especially for its flagship BTech course. CSE remained the most competitive branch with the General AI quota closing at 150. BTech AI & Data Analytics and BTech EE are the second & third most popular branches with 226 & 742 closing rank. Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering is the easiest to get in with a closing rank of 7837.
